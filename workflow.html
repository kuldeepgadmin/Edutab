How We Work & Execute Projects

A structured, transparent, 4-step model to ensure seamless system adoption.

The deployment model

Four steps, from first conversation to steady state

Nothing is configured before the workflow is understood, and nothing goes live before the office staff have used it themselves.

01

Requirement Assessment & Discovery

We review your institution's current workflow, existing data formats and primary pain points to define clear project goals without technical jargon.

02

System Customization & Setup

We explore, configure and adapt the chosen ERP platform or website modules, setting up prototypes for review and testing.

03

Data Migration & Final Launch

Data is securely formatted, validated and imported into the live system. We run rigorous tests to ensure error-free operation before launch.

04

Continuous Technical Support

Post-launch, we provide ongoing system maintenance, troubleshooting and continuous updates throughout the operational year.

What each step produces

Deliverables you can hold, at every stage

Each step ends with a written artefact that your management committee can review. If a phase does not produce one, it is not considered complete — and it is not billed.

Working principles

How the engagement behaves day to day

Single point of accountability

One named engineer owns your campus file. Escalations do not restart the explanation from scratch.

Academic-calendar aware

Changes, patches and go-lives are scheduled around exams, admissions and result declarations — never during them.

Written before executed

Scope, access changes and data operations are confirmed in writing. Verbal instructions are not actioned on live systems.

Terms of service & general guidelines

Clear terms, agreed before the first configuration

Service Scope

All systems, configurations and deliverables are built for your internal operations based on agreed scope parameters. Anything outside the scope sheet is quoted as an addendum before it is started.

Usage & Security

Clients retain ownership of all access credentials and assets, operating systems within established safety guidelines. Shared credentials are rotated at staff exit, and access is role-limited by default.

Flexibility

Engagement models are designed on a "pay-as-you-grow" basis to eliminate excessive upfront capital expenditure — phases can be paused at academic boundaries.

Standard assumptions for a campus engagement
  • One authorized institutional contact signs scope and go-live.
  • Hosting, domain and gateway accounts remain in the institution's name.
  • Data quality issues found during migration are reported, not silently patched.
  • Support window: Monday to Saturday, standard business hours.
  • Critical outage handling continues outside the window during exam periods.
  • Third-party license and gateway fees are billed at actuals to the institution.
